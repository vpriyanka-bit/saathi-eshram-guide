# Saathi — e-Shram in simple steps

A Hindi and English, phone-friendly guide to e-Shram registration for informal workers. The app offers four plain-language steps, browser read-aloud, optional voice questions, and a text question helper.

## Use

Open the website and select Hindi or English. Read or listen to each step. Ask a question by typing, or use the microphone if your browser supports speech recognition. The guide never asks for Aadhaar or OTP.

## Privacy and scope

This is an independent explainer, not a government service. It does not submit registration forms or collect personal information. The question helper uses fixed, reviewed answers and local keyword matching; it does not call a generative AI model. Optional speech recognition is provided by the browser and may process audio under its own terms.

Registration eligibility, documents, costs and help details can change. Check the official [e-Shram FAQ](https://eshram.gov.in/faqs). Online self-registration requires Aadhaar and an Aadhaar-linked mobile; a CSC or State Seva Kendra can help with biometric registration. Registration is free and does not guarantee cash benefits.
